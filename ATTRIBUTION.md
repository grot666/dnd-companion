## Reference entries and the series workbench (v4)

All Chinese entry text, theme essays and the example series are written for this project.

- Reading text is an independently worded Chinese summary of the cited sources, not a translation. Citations appear as footnotes at the end of each entry. This project's own commentary sits in a separate “解读” (interpretation) box. 2014 vs 2024 differences are stated in a “版本差异” section.
- Claims that could not be confirmed against a publicly readable first-hand source are **not** rendered in reading text. They are kept as author-facing drafts on the separate “核实草稿” page, each listing the leads found, the missing material and the blocker.
- Verification, licence, the author's adoption decision and visibility are tracked separately. Each source carries a licence label: CC BY 4.0 (may be paraphrased and cited) / official page (link and independent summary only) / copyrighted book (title only) / community content (link only).

### Illustrations

The 29 images in `public/art/` are original semi-realistic digital paintings generated for this project with an AI image tool in November 2026, then converted to WebP. They depict no real people, contain no text or real-world religious symbols, and are not official D&D artwork. Seven of them illustrate the example series. They are released with the project under the MIT license as far as any rights exist in them.

Icons use Lucide (ISC; portions from Feather under MIT). Runtime dependency license notices are preserved in `public/third-party-notices.txt`.
